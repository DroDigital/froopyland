Things to do

  • Discover places — the compass shows a pulsing ? towards the nearest signal. Each landmark does something: portals fold you across the world, monoliths and spires fill in your map, eggs hatch, fountains make you fast.
  • Observe residents — press Q on a creature to log its species, then watch it to record behaviours (eating, drinking, sleeping, fleeing, singing…).
  • Collect memory motes ✧ — every 3 unlock a fragment of the builder's log.
  • Pick glowfruit ● from bushes and feed residents to befriend them.
  • Decode the world's rules at terminals and monoliths — each seed has its own laws.
  • Mushroom caps bounce you. Rainbow rivers carry you. The edge of the world is a wall.
